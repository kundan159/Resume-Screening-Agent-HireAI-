Job Title: Backend Software Engineer (Python / Cloud)
Location: Bengaluru, India (Hybrid)
Experience Required: 4-8 years

About the Role:
We are looking for a Backend Software Engineer to design, build, and scale the services that
power our core product. You will work closely with product, frontend, and DevOps teams to
ship reliable, well-tested APIs and services running in the cloud.

Required Skills:
- Strong proficiency in Python (4+ years professional experience)
- Experience building and maintaining REST APIs (Flask, FastAPI, or Django)
- Solid understanding of relational databases, especially PostgreSQL, and SQL query optimization
- Hands-on experience with AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS)
- Experience with Docker and container-based deployments
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and Git-based workflows
- Understanding of system design and distributed systems fundamentals
- Experience writing unit and integration tests (pytest or similar)

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with Kubernetes
- Experience with message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ, or SQS)
- Familiarity with Redis or other caching layers
- Exposure to Terraform or other infrastructure-as-code tools
- Experience mentoring junior engineers or leading small projects
- Familiarity with GraphQL

Education:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent
  practical experience)

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement scalable backend services and APIs
- Own features end-to-end: design, implementation, testing, deployment, and monitoring
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define technical requirements
- Participate in code reviews and uphold engineering best practices
- Debug and resolve production issues, contributing to on-call rotations
- Continuously improve system performance, reliability, and security
